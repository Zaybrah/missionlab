                            Map

                            Example map

                            The lines around the walkable ground are borders nobody can cross: walls, fences, furniture. For real walls and furniture in the 3D view, also open the .mlgeo file that Game Master mode exports into BepInEx/MissionLab/maps (press M there). Open a map file from OPERATOR/OPERATOR_Data/NavMeshData. You can also open a scenario, a mission record from BepInEx/MissionLab/missions, or a recon file to see insertion points. Files stay on your computer.

                            Which map file belongs to which mission?
                            • Clean House (Suburb): suburbv2.bytes
                            • Depot Mexico: depotraid1.bytes
                            • Any other mission: host it once with MissionLab installed. The banner at the top left of the game names the file, and so does missions/last.json.

                            New mission: Intel raid

                            Five steps to a whole mission: the squad goes in, takes intel from a building and gets out. Make the mission writes ordinary triggers, enemies and host options that you change afterwards like any others, and a walkthrough test in Rehearse. One Undo takes all of it back.

                            1. The map's own insertions, from the game or the map's recon file.

                            2. Only intel this map is known to have. The game decides where it lies; the way out stays shut until it is taken.

                            3. A named place under Places. Walking into it tells the squad they are in the right building, and the garrison stands inside it.

                            4. Encounters (optional)

                                A kept module (Modules, Triggers tab) put down in a named place. Modules whose enemies hunt or rally are not offered: that has failed in the game.

                              • Pressure and your estimates

                                Both become host options with your pick as the default: the host can change them before each mission.

                                to

                                Length and squad are your estimate, written into the scenario as such; nobody has measured them.

                                Quick mission

                                MissionLab's generator makes a layout on a map: Generate shows it on the map, Reroll tries another seed, Keep opens it here as a new scenario to change and save.

                                The quick mission needs the game: the generator reads the map's own navigation file in your OPERATOR folder, which MissionLab never copies. Start OPERATOR with MissionLab and open http://localhost:8765.

                                Shares, not counts. All three at 0: the generator's own mix, with more guards indoors.

                                Moved intel is not offered: it needs the game itself and the RandomIntel setting, so the intel stays where the game puts it.

                                  Places

                                  Names for parts of the map, such as Rear stairs or Upstairs office. Triggers, the mission check and replays then say where something is in words. A point place covers 3 m around its pin on its floor; an area place is painted.

                                  Check the places

                                      Floors and rooms

                                      Work on one floor or room at a time. Focus brings it into view, Isolate shows only it, Hide leaves it off the map, and Lock keeps clicks, boxes and drags from moving anything on it. None of this is saved in the scenario: it is kept in this browser for this map file, and saving, export, the mission check and the game always include everything.

                                      Floors

                                        No floors yet. Choose a preset, or add one for the floor in the middle of the map.

                                        Buildings

                                          No buildings yet. A building is a set of Places, one per room.

                                          Apply a floor band

                                          Presets, bands and the order above change only this page. Apply is the one step here that changes the mission: every ticked area takes the chosen floor's band, and Undo takes it back.

                                          Section

                                            Replay

                                            How it went

                                              What happened

                                                Blue is a player, red an enemy; a filled red dot has noticed the players. Lines show the last half minute. Click an event to jump to it. The replay is drawn on the 2D map.

                                                Debrief

                                                Facts from the recording only: each number names the event or record it was counted from, and older recordings mark their guesses. Health, shots fired and players' use of doors are not recorded, so they are never given.

                                                              Modules

                                                                      Objectives 
                                                                      
    
                                                                      What the players are asked to do, in order. Each objective is completed when a trigger fires and can fail by another; it becomes active once the objectives it waits for are complete (and its own trigger, if you choose one, has fired). Players see "New objective", "Objective complete" and "Objective failed" messages. These are not the intel items on the map.
                                                                      
    
                                                                        
    
                                                                        Add a trigger first: an objective is completed when one fires.
                                                                        
    
                                                                        
    
    
                                                                          
    
                                                                          A mission is running: these are its objectives, as the game started it with them. They cannot be changed until it ends.
                                                                          
  
                                                                          

  
                                                                          
    
                                                                          Intel items
                                                                          
    
                                                                          Amber markers are the mission's intel items. Drag one to move it; in 3D you can put it on a table or shelf. The game lays each item in one of a few places, a different one each mission; an item you move always lies where you put it.
                                                                          
    
                                                                          
      
                                                                            
    
                                                                            
  
                                                                            
  
                                                                            
    
                                                                            Rehearse
                                                                            
    
                                                                            
      
                                                                            Plays this scenario's triggers on a simulated clock through events you write here, with the same trigger rules the game uses, and checks what should follow. Every event is an assumption you inject: a rehearsal does not predict spotting, pathfinding, physics, networking or combat.
                                                                            
      
                                                                            
      
                                                                            
      
                                                                            
      
                                                                            
      
                                                                            
        
                                                                            Inside or outside?
                                                                            
        
                                                                            Probe a point at an exact height: every zone and hold trigger says whether it counts as inside, and why: which part of its area takes it, which hole leaves it out, which floor bound it misses, or how far it is from an old circle's anchor.
                                                                            
        
                                                                            
          
          
          
        
                                                                            
        
                                                                            
          
          
          
        
                                                                            
        
                                                                              
        
                                                                              Walk it through
                                                                              
        
                                                                              Give up to 4 test players timed paths: they walk in straight lines between waypoints at an even speed, stand at the first waypoint until its time and stay at the last. The rehearsal samples where they are every 0.5 s, as the game's trigger checks do, and plays those places with the events and checks in the rows below. Two waypoints at one time are a jump.
                                                                              
        
                                                                              
        
                                                                              
          
          
        
                                                                              
        
                                                                                
        
                                                                                
          
          
        
                                                                                
        
                                                                                
        
        
                                                                                
        
                                                                                
        
                                                                                Between two samples the rehearsal sees nothing. A path that goes through a zone, or out of it and back, between two samples is listed here for you to add a waypoint; it never counts on its own.
                                                                                
        
                                                                                  
        
                                                                                  
          
          
          
        
                                                                                  
        
                                                                                  A replay shows only where players walked. Intel picked up, doors opened, enemies going down, the alarm and deaths are not taken from it: add them yourself as rows.

                                                                                      Host options 
                                                                                      
    
                                                                                      Choices the host makes before the mission, such as how many guards or how many reinforcement waves. A choice can drop enemies of a lot, switch triggers off, and set waves and enemy skill. Name choices for what they do; how hard one plays is your suggestion until it has been measured.

                                                                                            Pressure director (experiment)
                                                                                            
        
                                                                                            The director plays only the cards above, through the budget and their own placement rules, and never changes how the mission is completed. It looks at the time, active objectives and the living players and enemies the game sees. Where reinforcements appear is checked by the cards' distance and view test, an approximation of what players see. Older MissionLab versions ignore it and play without pacing.
                                                                                            
        
        
                                                                                            
          
                                                                                            
          
                                                                                            
          
                                                                                            
          
                                                                                            Bands are tried in order; the first whose conditions hold decides how much may be spent and how often.
